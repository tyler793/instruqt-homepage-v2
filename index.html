The platform

Real infrastructure, not a simulation layer.

Build labs in hours on the stack your product runs on, share them with any audience, and see what hands-on time does to your pipeline.

Any stack

VMs, containers, Kubernetes clusters, disposable cloud accounts, GPUs, and managed AI services like Bedrock and Vertex. Nothing gets cut from the demo because the platform couldn’t run it.

Sandboxed & disposable

Every session gets its own isolated cloud account with scoped, short-lived credentials. No shared state, no route to production. Pause it, resume it, or throw it away, with nothing to clean up afterwards.

Labs-as-code / GitOps

Labs can live as code. Keep them in Git, review changes in PRs, and validate them in CI. When the product changes, the lab fails your pipeline instead of your customer demo.

AI-assisted authoring

Create sandbox and instructional content from your docs, repos, or a brain dump. A working lab in an afternoon, and every team ships in the same brand and voice.

Embeddable anywhere

Drop labs into landing pages, campaigns, docs, or your LMS over LTI. No account, no install, no download. One click and your audience is in the product instead of reading about it.

Analytics & tracking

Engagement data to understand your users and improve your content. The same signals in Salesforce or HubSpot to show what hands-on time did for pipeline.
